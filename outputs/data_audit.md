## 7. Preprocessing decisions

- Official train/dev/test splits are used as released; nothing is re-split or filtered.
- Stored text keeps the original casing, punctuation and negation; only outer whitespace is stripped.
- Chronological order: MELD rows are ordered by Utterance_ID inside each Dialogue_ID; EmoryNLP keeps the JSON order inside each scene. `turn_index` records the resulting position.
- Dialogue = MELD Dialogue_ID / EmoryNLP scene. Context never crosses a dialogue boundary.
- MELD joint speakers ("Joey and Chandler") are split into a speaker list; group names such as "All" and "Both" stay literal speaker ids, so they only match other "All"/"Both" turns.
- Labels are lower-cased and mapped with a fixed alphabetical order per dataset; history labels are never part of model inputs.
- MELD train: 72 dialogues have gaps in Utterance_ID (utterances removed by the MELD authors); ids are kept as released and order follows the remaining ids.
- MELD dev: 2 dialogues have gaps in Utterance_ID (utterances removed by the MELD authors); ids are kept as released and order follows the remaining ids.
- MELD test: 2 dialogues have gaps in Utterance_ID (utterances removed by the MELD authors); ids are kept as released and order follows the remaining ids.
